
# chrome_store

#### publish your extension 
* You can publish your extension by uploading a compressed ZIP file of your code to the Chrome Web Store Developer Dashboard.
  * https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/publish

#### Developer Account
* Create a Developer Account Go to the Chrome Web Store Developer Dashboard and sign in with your Google account.
  * Accept the developer agreement and privacy policy.Pay the one-time registration fee of $5 USD.
  * https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/publish
  * https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UWSxfcWHejY
  * https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7BbNHBucSIg&t=133s

#### Prepare and Package
* Prepare and Package Your Extension Test your extension locally in Google Chrome under developer mode to ensure it works.
* Include a valid manifest.json file with your extension's name, description, version, and icons.
* Compress your extension's source folder into a standard .zip file (maximum size is 2GB).
  * https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/publish
  * https://dev.to/artem_turlenko/how-to-publish-your-chrome-extension-on-the-chrome-web-store-3p3e
  * https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MPzh5KrB_TI&t=1s
  * https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7BbNHBucSIg&t=133s

#### Upload and Fill Store Listing Details
* Click Add new item (or New Item) on the dashboard and upload your ZIP file.
* Fill out the Store Listing tab with your title, detailed description, category, language, and high-resolution icons/screenshots.
* Fill out the Privacy tab by providing a privacy policy URL, a single-purpose description, and justifications for your requested permissions.
  * https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k8MZbEK-Kyw&t=2s
  * https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/publish
  * https://rxliuli.com/blog/browser-extension-dev-08-publishing-to-chrome-web-store/
  * https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/publish

#### Submit for Review
* Click Submit for review once all required sections are completed.
* Wait for Google's review process, which typically takes 2 to 3 days before your extension goes live.
